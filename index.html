Our Story

Street Food. No Walls.

ONI brings Japanese-inspired street food to Louisiana through pop-up events at bars, breweries, and venues across Baton Rouge and New Orleans. From hand-formed onigiri to loaded JapDogs, every item is built for flavor and built for the moment. No storefront. No reservations. Just bold food showing up where the energy is.
